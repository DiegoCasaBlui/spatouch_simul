## Findings

No actionable P0/P1/P2 findings in the final comparison.

- **Typography:** existing Arial family and cyan panel text retained. CMS title, SN, code and scan instruction follow the reference hierarchy. Slight font weight differences are expected because the exact device font is unavailable.
- **Spacing/layout:** centered QR, serial/code above it, two-line instruction below, persistent left navigation and top-right Back control. All keyboard rows and confirmation controls fit the fixed panel at desktop and phone scale.
- **Colors/tokens:** uses the simulator's existing blue background texture, cyan controls and selected Wi-Fi tab. The brighter/more textured background in the new photos is intentionally not substituted for the established PDF-based shell.
- **Assets:** reuses existing simulator icons and texture. The QR is generated by qrcode.react with a four-module quiet zone; it contains demo JSON rather than copying a real registration QR. This intentional content difference changes the QR pattern. No decorative image placeholders were introduced.
- **Copy/content:** exact requested registration question and Control My Spa label. CMS heading, SN, CMS field and final instruction follow the references. Network names and serial number are fictional. Demo-only behavior is explained outside the panel in Reference and scope and in README.
